Choose what happens to an app on your display when this task ends. By default every app you opened stays open in the background on the phone, on the page where you left it (the user finds it in recent apps, and the next task continues from there), except an app that is playing sound or video at that moment, which is closed. Mark keep when the user wants the app to keep running after you are done (keep playing the music or the video), even while it plays. Mark close when the user asks you to close it, or when leaving it open makes no sense (a one-time sign-in or a page you opened only to check something). It only marks the app; nothing changes now. Marking the same app again replaces the earlier mark.
